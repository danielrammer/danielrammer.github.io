From 2007 to 2012, I worked on multiple custom automation and robotics projects across various industries. After mastering the fundamentals of machine and system assembly and wiring, I specialized in the simulation, programming, and testing of robotic systems. This work often included the integration of vision systems for tracking and quality assurance. I valued the technical challenges, the continual exposure to new technologies, and the opportunity to travel extensively (Europe and USA)

## Focus and Tasks
Robotic programming, testing, simulation, installation
